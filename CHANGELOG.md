# Changelog

## 0.9.1 — 2026-09-11

Prototype archive and integration checkpoint. Direction paused for redesign after feedback about unclear purpose and insufficient engagement.

- Preserve Type0 habit world, Type1 concept review, Type2 city, and Type3 ecology as distinct experiments.
- Include earlier colony, caravan, defense, and salvage source and tests.
- Extend Type3 with three exploration stages, ponds, water-carrying beetles, pollinating moths, moss nesting, moonstone, four flower colors, renewable seeds, inherited snail traits, and growth.
- Keep inventory to three visible items per page and contextual one-line information.
- Preserve object identity when packed, migrate old garden saves, and support version 11 diagnostic replay.
- Consolidate prototype documentation and correct stale conversation-only launch/release guidance.
- Align portable packaging and Windows metadata with the project version.

These are experimental systems, not validated claims of fun or playtime. Runtime saves and playtest artifacts are not part of the source release.
