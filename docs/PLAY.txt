Unseen Order 0.9.1 — prototype archive checkpoint

This is an experimental collection, not a finished game. The default is Type3, A World, Gathered. Its design is paused for reconsideration.

Run Unseen-Order.exe. Keep the PCK and data directory beside the executable.
Drag objects to rearrange the garden. Use the lower pouch buttons to place things; arrows browse three items at a time. Turn off the lights to warm stones. After growing a flower, click the hollow to send an expedition. Select an object to pack it without losing its traits.

Space: pause/resume. Escape: cancel selection. F8: record a feedback marker. The upper language button switches English/Korean.

Saves are local in %APPDATA%/UnseenOrder. The active garden uses type3-garden-v11.json and can import the earlier v10 save without deleting it. Detailed local playtest recording is enabled at the first player's explicit request; game-only captures and logs are written under artifacts in the package folder. No live AI requests are made by the garden.

Earlier source prototypes, tests, and design notes are preserved in the repository. No Steam integration or mobile build is included.
