UNSEEN ORDER — Conversation prototype 0.3.0

Run Unseen-Order.exe. Keep all files and the data directory together.
No Godot editor or .NET SDK installation is needed.

Choose English or Korean at the top right.
Walk to a nearby place. Choose someone within earshot. Ask, listen,
compare stories, or make a small request. Let time pass and return.
The world runs without AI. Space pauses it. The notebook stores your notes.
Scroll the conversation choices to see additional options.

Optional AI: Notebook / AI > AI voice.
Requires your own OpenAI API key. The model is fixed to GPT-5.6 Luna.
Usage may incur charges. The key stays in memory for this session.
Without AI, typed questions use topic matching; buttons expose all actions.

Saves: %APPDATA%/UnseenOrder/conversation-v3.json
An automatic backup is kept beside the save.

This is a small local prototype. No BGM, Steam integration, or installer.


MORE TO TALK ABOUT
Ask whether a neighbor needs a hand. Promises appear in the notebook and may involve another person or a later visit. Look around at a place to notice details. Rest until morning from Your notes when you want to move to another day. Returning to people can open more personal conversations.

FIRST-PLAYER RECORDING
Detailed play history is saved locally in the game's user data folder under playtests. It includes conversation input, notes, choices, world state changes and simulation decisions for development. API keys are never recorded. Press F8 to mark a surprising, confusing or repetitive moment and describe what you expected. No telemetry is uploaded.
